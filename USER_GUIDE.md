# Kids Coloring Book - User Guide & Activity Handbook

Welcome to **Kids Coloring Book**, a vibrant, lightweight, and offline Windows desktop application designed especially for children, parents, and teachers!

---

## 1. Main Dashboard

When the game launches, you will see a cheerful dashboard with 6 big, colorful tiles:

1. **🖍 Start Coloring:** Browse 300 unique black-and-white sketches divided into Easy, Medium, and Hard difficulty levels.
2. **🖼 My Drawings:** View, open, continue editing, rename, delete, or print your saved artworks.
3. **✏ Draw Your Own:** Create original drawings on a blank canvas with freehand pencils, straight lines, rectangles, circles, stars, and hearts!
4. **🖨 Print Gallery:** Quickly print original black-and-white coloring sheets or your completed coloured artworks.
5. **⚙ Settings:** Set your artist name (for print signatures), toggle full-screen mode, switch sound effects on/off, and check storage usage.
6. **🚪 Exit App:** Closes the game safely.

---

## 2. Choosing a Sketch (300 Unique Sketches)

Click **Start Coloring** to open the Sketch Gallery:
- **Level 1: Easy (100 Sketches):** Bold, simple outlines with large coloring regions. Perfect for younger children.
  - *Categories:* Fruits, Vegetables, Balloons, Flowers, Butterflies, Fish, Birds, Sun & Sky, Basic Animals, Treats & Toys.
- **Level 2: Medium (100 Sketches):** Medium detail with multiple coloring sections.
  - *Categories:* Houses & Castles, Cars & Transport, Trains & Ships, Dinosaurs, Cartoon Animals, Trees, Gardens, Toys & Robots, Farm Life, Sports & Music.
- **Level 3: Hard (100 Sketches):** Intricate illustrations and detailed patterns.
  - *Categories:* Detailed Animals, Mandala Patterns, Landscapes, Underwater Scenes, City & Architecture, Complex Botanicals, Space & Tech, Fantasy & Myth, Geometric Art, Festive & Cultural Art.

**Tip:** Use the category pills at the top or the Search bar (e.g., search *"Apple"* or *"Dino"*) to quickly find favorite sketches!

---

## 3. Coloring Workspace

When you select a sketch, the interactive coloring studio opens:

### 🪣 Fill Colour Tool (Recommended for Magic Coloring!)
- Click the **Fill** tool on the left panel.
- Choose a colour from the right palette.
- Click anywhere inside a shape: the area fills with vibrant color instantly while keeping the crisp black outline intact!

### ✏ Pencil Tool (Freehand Drawing)
- Click the **Pencil** tool.
- Draw freehand lines using the mouse or touchscreen.
- Adjust pencil thickness using the **Thin**, **Med**, **Thick**, **Jumbo** buttons or slider on the right panel.

### 🧹 Eraser Tool
- Click the **Eraser** tool to erase unwanted strokes cleanly back to white.

### ↩ Undo & ↪ Redo
- Made a mistake? Click **Undo** to step backward (up to 20 steps).
- Click **Redo** to restore actions.

### 🔄 Clear & Reset
- **Clear All:** Clears your current coloring strokes.
- **Reset Sketch:** Requests confirmation before resetting back to the pristine original outline.

### 🔍 Zoom & Pan
- Use the **Zoom In (🔍+)**, **Zoom Out (🔍-)**, and **Fit (⛶)** buttons at the top right for precision coloring on small details.

---

## 4. Colour Palette & Custom Colours

- **17+ Predefined Child-Friendly Shades:** Red, Blue, Green, Yellow, Orange, Pink, Purple, Brown, Black, White, Grey, Light Blue, Dark Blue, Light Green, Dark Green, Skin Tone, Coral, Lime, Teal, Lavender, and more.
- **🎨 Custom Colour Picker:** Click the *Custom Colour...* button to mix your own colors with sliders or pick from quick-palette presets.
- **Recent Colours:** The top 8 most recently used colors are saved in a quick-access row.

---

## 5. Printing Your Artwork

Printing is easy and supports both physical printers and PDF export:

### Option 1: Print Coloured Sketch
- Click **Print Coloured** on the bottom action bar.
- Enter or edit the child's name (e.g. *"Rohit"*).
- Today's date is automatically added (*"Date: DD/MM/YYYY"*).
- The print layout centers your colorful drawing in a framed A4 format.
- Click **🖨 Print Now** to send to your printer, **📄 Save as PDF** to create a PDF file, or **🖼 Save as Image (PNG)**.

### Option 2: Print Original Sketch
- Click **Print Original** to print the clean, black-and-white outline.
- Perfect for printing physical coloring sheets for coloring with real crayons, colored pencils, or markers!

---

## 6. Draw Your Own Sketch

Click **Draw Your Own** from the main dashboard:
- Starts with a clean 800x800 blank canvas.
- Includes **Pencil**, **Eraser**, and 5 shape tools:
  - 📏 Straight Line
  - ⬛ Rectangle
  - ⚪ Circle / Ellipse
  - ⭐ 5-Point Star
  - 💖 Heart Shape
- Click **Save to Gallery** to store your custom drawing in *My Drawings*.

---

## 7. My Drawings Gallery

- Displays all your saved artwork with thumbnails, creation date, and titles.
- Click **Edit** to reopen and continue coloring where you left off.
- Click **Rename** to personalize the title.
- Click **Print** to open the print preview with signature and date.
- Click **Delete** (with confirmation dialog) to clean up old artwork.
